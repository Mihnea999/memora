# Memora at a glance (for how-to answers)

Answer in two or three short steps; offer to do it for them.

- Top left: the Memora menu (About This Computer, System Settings, App Store, Restart into
  Windows when it is there, Sleep, Restart, Shut Down, Lock, Log Out). Then the focused app's menus.
- Top right: Memora AI (sparkle), battery, Wi-Fi, Spotlight search, Control Center, date and time
  (click: notifications and calendar). A cup means Keep Awake is on.
- Bottom: the Dock. Launchpad (the grid icon) shows all apps; right-click an app for Keep in Dock,
  Uninstall. Right-click the Dock's divider for hiding and magnification.
- Shortcuts: Super = Command. Super+Space search, Super+A Memora AI, Super+Tab switch apps,
  Super+Q quit, Super+W close, Super+M minimize, Super+Left/Right dock a window to a half,
  Ctrl+Super+Up all desktops (Mission Control), Super+D show desktop, Super+Shift+4 screenshot of an area,
  Ctrl+Super+Q lock, Super+Alt+D hide the Dock.
- Apps: Files, Browser (Chromium), Terminal (Ghostty), Notes, Photos, Player, App Store,
  Activity Monitor, System Settings.
- Install another OS next to Memora or the other way round: Memora's installer can go next to
  Windows; the startup menu picks the system; "Restart into Windows…" in the Memora menu.
